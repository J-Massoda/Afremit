Before you join

Questions about
the pilot.

Clear expectations help every school, provider, payer, and partner decide whether a guided test is useful.

Can my institution start without uploading its full fee catalogue?

Yes. An approved provider can begin with one fee, service estimate, or construction milestone. A full catalogue is not required for the pilot.

Does Afremit take or hold real money today?

No. Payments, held balances, allocations, and refunds are simulated records. There is no live pay-in, payout, currency conversion, or escrow custody in this release.

Are participating institutions financially verified?

No. Afremit staff approve access to test the pilot workflow. That decision is not a substitute for the due diligence required before any live payment service.

What information should we use during testing?

Start with fictional or minimal business information. Do not enter patient records, children's names, identity numbers, card details, bank credentials, or real payment instructions.

What does a family member abroad see?

After receiving a private request code, they can review its purpose, amount, and reference, complete a test transaction, and follow its recorded outcome. The provider submits evidence and an Afremit reviewer records the simulated release decision.

How can a provider or partner take part?

Providers can apply in the pilot workspace. For a guided test, technical integration, or partnership conversation, reach Afremit through its LinkedIn business page. Public pilot registration will be available when the connected deployment is configured.
